## COLD OPEN

**EXT. KASUMI ISLAND — NIGHT**
*A sky crowded with stars. We tilt down past the moon to the sea and a red-and-white
lighthouse on a cliff. Its beam sweeps slowly over the water.*

> **MIO (V.O.):** On Kasumi Island, the old fishermen say that every falling star is a wish somebody gave up on.
>
> **MIO (V.O.):** I never believed them. Not until the night one fell into my net.

**EXT. LIGHTHOUSE GALLERY — CONTINUOUS**
*MIO TSUKISHIMA (16, yellow raincoat, star hair clip) leans on the iron railing, gazing up.*

> **MIO:** Grandpa! The stars are so bright tonight. Do you think they ever get lonely up there?

**INT. LIGHTHOUSE — DOORWAY**
*GRANDPA GENZO, cap and white moustache, chuckles in the lamplight.*

> **GENZO:** Lonely? Ha! They've got each other. Now come inside, Mio. There's a storm coming in from the east.

*Mio pouts, blushing.*

> **MIO:** Just one more minute...

*A single star flickers... swells... then breaks loose and streaks across the sky.*

> **MIO:** Huh? That star... It's moving!

*WIDE: the falling star dives into the cove below the cliff. FLASH. A column of water,
a ring of light racing across the sea. The cove keeps glowing.*

> **MIO:** It fell... It fell right into the cove!

*She bolts out of frame.*

## OPENING — "STARFALL KEEPER"

**Episode 1 — The Star in the Net**

## PART A — THE BEACH

**EXT. KASUMI BEACH — NIGHT**
*Mio runs along the surf with a lantern.*

> **MIO:** Haah... haah... It should be right around here...

*Grandpa's old fishing net lies tangled between the rocks, pulsing with soft light.*

> **MIO:** Grandpa's old fishing net... Is it glowing?
>
> **MIO:** It's... warm.

*A pillar of light rises from the net. Inside it, a girl floats upward — long silver
hair drifting like water, eyes closed. Her eyes open: gold, with star-shaped pupils.
This is LUMI.*

> **LUMI:** Is this... the bottom of the sky?
>
> **MIO:** *(flustered)* The bottom of the... what? This is Kasumi Beach! Are you okay? Did you fall off a boat?
>
> **LUMI:** *(a sad little smile)* A boat? No... I fell off of everything.

*Her glow fades. She sinks onto the net and collapses. Mio runs to her.*

> **MIO:** Hey! Hey, hang on! ... GRANDPAAA!

## PART B — THE LIGHTHOUSE

**INT. LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER'S ROOM — LATER**
*The storm has arrived. Rain against the porthole. Lumi sits wrapped in a patchwork
blanket. Genzo studies her.*

> **GENZO:** Silver hair... eyes like starlight. In fifty years of tending this light, I've only ever heard of them in stories.
>
> **MIO:** Stories? What stories?
>
> **GENZO:** The Starfolk. When a star loses its way, it falls to the earth, searching for a light that can guide it home.
>
> **LUMI:** Your light... I heard it, all the way from up there. It was the only warm thing in the dark.
>
> **MIO:** *(fired up)* Then we'll help you get back! Right, Grandpa?
>
> **GENZO:** Hmm... The lighthouse only shines across the sea, Mio. To turn it toward the sky, you would need...

*Lumi's glow flickers.*

> **LUMI:** I don't have much time. If my light goes out before I return... I'll turn into an ordinary stone.
>
> **MIO:** A stone?!

*Lightning. Thunder.*

## PART C — THE PROMISE

**EXT. LIGHTHOUSE GALLERY — LATER THAT NIGHT**
*The storm has passed. The two girls side by side at the railing.*

> **MIO:** Hey, Lumi... What's it like? Being a star?
>
> **LUMI:** Quiet. Beautiful. And very, very far away from everyone.
>
> **MIO:** Well... You're not far away now.

*INSERT: Mio's hand reaches for Lumi's. Fingertips touch — a burst of light.*

> **LUMI:** *(a tear, smiling)* Mio... your hand is so warm. Is this what a wish feels like?
>
> **MIO:** I promise, Lumi. I'll get you home.

*WIDE: the lighthouse turning in the dark. On the gallery, two tiny lights: one gold, one silver.*

## TAG

**EXT. A SHIP IN A CRIMSON SKY**
*A hooded figure, THE COLLECTOR, holds a lantern full of caged, struggling stars. Red eyes.*

> **COLLECTOR:** One of my stars has slipped out of its cage...
>
> **COLLECTOR:** Find her. Before the seventh night is over, her light will be mine.

*The eyes flare. Black.*
